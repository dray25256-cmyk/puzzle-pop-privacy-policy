Puzzle Pop Privacy Policy

Developer: SANJOY APPS • Last Updated: September 30, 2026
1

Introduction

Welcome to Puzzle Pop, a mobile sliding tile puzzle game developed by SANJOY APPS. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through our compliance with this policy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you download, install, and enjoy the Puzzle Pop game on Android devices.

2

Information We Collect

Puzzle Pop is designed as an offline-first casual game that respects user privacy:

  • No User Account: Puzzle Pop does not require you to create an account, register, or log in.
  • No Personal Contact Information: We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, physical address, or telephone number to play the game.
  • Local Storage: Your game progression (unlocked levels, star ratings, fastest completion times, and audio preferences) is stored locally on your device via standard Android application storage.
3

Advertising and Google AdMob

Puzzle Pop uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements and full-screen interstitial advertisements.

Google and its advertising technology partners may automatically collect, process, and use certain device-level information to deliver, target, and measure ads in compliance with Google's policies. This information may include:

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / GAID)
  • Approximate geographical location inferred from IP address
  • Device details such as device model, manufacturer, and operating system version
  • Ad interaction metrics including impressions, viewability, and ad engagement

To learn more about how Google processes advertising information, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4

How Information Is Used

Any information processed through Google AdMob is utilized for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Delivering banner and interstitial advertisements within the application
  • Measuring ad performance, impressions, and click-through rates
  • Preventing advertising fraud, abuse, and invalid traffic
  • Ensuring technical stability and compatibility across Android devices
5

Third-Party Services

Puzzle Pop integrates Google AdMob as its sole third-party service provider. We do not incorporate any other external analytics frameworks, social network plugins, tracking pixels, or third-party data broker SDKs.

6

Data Sharing

SANJOY APPS does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information handled by third-party advertising services (Google AdMob) is processed according to Google's respective privacy practices, terms of service, and applicable data protection regulations.

7

Data Security

We employ reasonable technical and administrative precautions to maintain application security. Because Puzzle Pop does not maintain user account registries or collect sensitive credentials, risks associated with centralized data breaches are significantly minimized. However, please note that no electronic transmission or digital storage method is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8

Data Retention and Deletion

Because Puzzle Pop does not provide or require user accounts, SANJOY APPS does not maintain a user account database for account deletion.

All gameplay progress, star records, and settings are saved locally on your device. You may delete all local game data at any time by:

  • Using the in-game Reset Level Progress option found in the Settings dialog, or
  • Clearing the app storage/cache via Android device Settings → Apps → Puzzle Pop → Storage → Clear Data, or
  • Uninstalling the game from your Android device.

Data processed by third-party advertising services is subject to Google's data retention and deletion policies.

9

Children's Privacy

Puzzle Pop is a general-audience sliding puzzle game. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that personal data from a child has been collected inadvertently through third-party advertising services, please contact us immediately so that we can take appropriate steps to investigate and rectify the situation.

10

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or technical changes. Any revisions will be published within the application and on this page. When updates occur, the Last Updated date at the top of this policy will be revised. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
